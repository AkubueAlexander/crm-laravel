# 13.3 HRIS integration decision

**Decision: payroll, benefits and tax compliance are a non-goal. We integrate outward, we do not rebuild.**

Per the blueprint (13.3), a compliance-heavy domain is handled by an external HRIS
(Gusto, Rippling or BambooHR). The CRM keeps only what supports the sales org:
employees, teams, territories and leave requests (13.1, 13.2).

## What exists
- `App\Domain\Hr\Hris\HrisAdapter`: read-only contract (`name()`, `fetchEmployees()`).
- `HrisEmployeeRecord`: DTO for one external employee.
- `NullHrisAdapter`: default; returns nothing.
- `HrisManager`: picks the adapter from `config('services.hris.driver')` (default `null`).

## What does not exist (on purpose)
- No payroll, pay rates, benefits, tax or time-tracking tables.
- No UI, routes, jobs or migrations for HRIS. Nothing is synced.
- No write-back to the HRIS.

## When a provider is wanted
1. Implement `HrisAdapter` for the provider (API calls server-side only, credentials in env/config).
2. Add a case to `HrisManager::adapter()`.
3. Add a queued job that maps `HrisEmployeeRecord` to `employees` through `SaveEmployeeAction` (match by email).
4. Decide then whether leave approvals should be pushed to the HRIS.
